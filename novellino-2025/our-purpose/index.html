Craft

Achieving the rich, sweet taste Filipinos deserve is the most difficult and costly choice we make.

We Control the Source

We are the only local maker to source grapes directly from farmers across three continents, securing the highest quality material and ensuring our supply is never out of stock.

Global Quality

We invest in state-of-the-art Italian equipment and a proprietary system that precisely arrests fermentation, locking in the consistent, delightful sweetness you love.

We Invest for Growth

We continue to expand our infrastructure and production capacity to meet growing demand, ensuring we consistently deliver fantastic Filipino wine in the Philippines and beyond.

Culture

Our purpose extends beyond the bottle; it is about building a richer life for our community. We believe in elevating shared moments and fueling the local economy.

We Redefine Tradition

We redefine the way Filipinos consume wine. Christmas isn't complete without Novellino on the table, and we're here for every moment, from grand wedding receptions to impromptu movie nights.

We Fuel the Economy

By importing grape juice and controlling the entire production process, we are putting the value in the Philippines, providing meaningful work and economic stability to many Filipinos.

We Deepen Connections

We seek to build and deepen ties between friends and family, making it easier to share joy not just during holidays, but in the intimate, everyday moments that matter most.

Conservation

It’s important we honor the environment from which we receive so much rich material. Our commitment to sustainability is driven by the desire to leave a better world for the next generation of Filipinos.

Wastewater Solutions

We pioneered eco-safe wastewater solutions now shared with global partners. Winemaking creates difficult wastewater, and we’re the only winery with a sustainable system that treats it safely with zero environmental impact.

Sustainable Power

We actively minimize our footprint by utilizing solar panels in our Calamba facility, ensuring our continued growth is power by clean and renewable resources.
