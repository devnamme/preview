Craft

The Process That Perfected Sweet

Achieving this rich, sweet taste is difficult and costly—but your expectation is worth it. We invest in state-of-the-art Italian equipment to precisely control fermentation, securing the flawless, sweet flavor you love every time.

Culture

The People's Choice

We've redefined how Filipinos enjoy wine. From Noche Buena to simple movie nights, Novellino is the sparkling joy that helps you focus on connecting with the people you love. We're building a shared movement together.

Conservation

Honoring Our Environment

Our commitment extends beyond the bottle. We utilize solar energy and have pioneered a revolutionary, Philippines-developed wastewater solution to ensure our growth is sustainable—protecting the environment for the next generation of Filipinos.

Our Legacy of Quality

For years, Filipinos felt wine was either too foreign, too expensive, or too confusing. Our founder saw that as an opportunity to create something truly ours. Since 1999, Novellino has been on a singular mission: to make delicious, approachable wine that makes everyone feel welcome.
